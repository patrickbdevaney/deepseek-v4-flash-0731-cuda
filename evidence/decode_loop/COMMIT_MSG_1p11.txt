WHAT CHANGED. kernels/compressed_decode.cu. compressed_verify_step_indexer forks the two
compressor_emit_group calls onto g_side and joined them straight back, immediately after build_qKV.
Nothing between there and index_score reads what the emits write: the first consumer of idx_ckv is

what let both arms be one build. ADOPTED, DEFAULT ON.

rate predicts -0.93 ms/forward.

WHAT IT MEANS, AND IT IS NOT THE 0.54. Run the standard way -- control arm first, so drift penalises
the change -- this measured -0.324, 2 SE [-0.670, +0.022]: a band covering zero, which under the
item's own pre-registration is a NEGATIVE RESULT and would have been written up as one. It is not
one. Pairing removes between-LEG variance and does nothing to a constant offset between two
checkpoint loads, and traps SS19 had already measured that offset at 0.6 % = ~0.8 ms/forward -- the
size of the entire effect. Running the identical pair with the ARM ORDER REVERSED gives
-0.760 [-1.109, -0.411]; averaging the per-leg deltas cancels the drift and half their difference
measures it: THE SECOND CHECKPOINT LOAD OF A SESSION COSTS +0.218 +/- 0.157 ms/forward, 40 % of what
this item was chasing. Term A needs another 22.4 ms and there is no single 22 ms item left, so it
will be paid down in pieces this size. Below ~1 ms/forward, a null from one arm order is not a
result, and reporting one retires a real win. tools/paired_band.py --reversed does the pooling.

a AND b ARE UNCHANGED AT 125.11 AND 1.887, ON PURPOSE. The item pre-registered a FLAT saving --
every byte in the deferred window is context-independent, and the mark-level confirmation was taken
at context FIVE. The engine did not deliver a flat one: -1.092 +/- 0.146 at ctx 12,410 (sd 0.179)
against bands covering zero at 3,197 and 6,260 (sd 0.585, 0.741). A three-point fit through groups
with 3-4x different scatter is not an attribution, so the ratchet is stated where it resolves --
a + b*ctx at ctx 12,410 goes 142.24 -> 141.15 ms -- and the term is new item 1.13. The item says its
own prediction failed rather than rounding it into the nearest term.
