WHAT SHIPPED. include/topk_radix.h: MSB-first 8-bit radix select over a 64-bit composite key
comp(v,t) = (ord(v) << 32) | ~t, one gather, one bitonic sort of the <= 512 winners. All four
kernels take it (k_topk_verify, k_topk_decode, k_topk_masked, k_topk_offset). O(T) work in a
constant number of passes instead of `topk` SEQUENTIAL argmax rounds -- 14-34x standalone, 18.0x at
T=3072 (ctx 12,288). DSV4_TOPK_RADIX=0 restores the warp scan exactly.

Because ~t makes every composite distinct there are no ties to break, so the select needs no
equal-key special case. Two details are load-bearing and each has its own gate distribution:
admission is the original's FLOAT compare v > floorv and never a key-space test (ord(NaN) beats
every finite key, but NaN > best is false); and -0.0 is folded onto +0.0 before the key is formed
(-0.0f == +0.0f is a tie for the original, but ord(-0.0) < ord(+0.0) as raw bits, and index_score
can emit -0.0).

BIT-EXACTNESS. Order is load-bearing, not just the set: sparse_attn sums the selected rows in order,
