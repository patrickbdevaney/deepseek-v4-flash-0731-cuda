THE MECHANISM, and the part worth remembering. The shipped warp kernel re-read both operands from
global on every head: 1.2 GB moved per call at the verify shape to do 151 M MACs, 0.5 FLOP/byte.
An intermediate tiled kernel fixes exactly that and is bit-identical to the shipped kernel -- and
that capped it at 2.0x, because bit-exactness with the warp kernel mandates keeping its 5-step
__shfl_down_sync tree, and SHFL is a ~200 us floor at this shape however the operands are staged.
The GEMM is bit-identical to index_score_kernel, the SCALAR REFERENCE gate_units checks against
ref/goldens, because register-tiled accumulation in k IS the reference's order -- and it is 6.8x.
"Bit-exact" is a two-place relation and the second argument is a design decision; claiming it
against the incumbent inherits every reassociation the incumbent ever made, including (Finding 68)
ones adopted as explicit deviations.
