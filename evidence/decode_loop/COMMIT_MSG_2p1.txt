2.1: the speculation block goes 6 -> 5, +3.91 +/- 1.65 % tok/s on 32 prompts AT EQUAL tau -- and making the verify expensive moved the optimum DOWN, not up

WHAT CHANGED. One constant, in the two places that own it: EngineConfig::blk in
include/dsv4_engine.h (the SERVED default -- scripts/serve.sh passes no --blk) and defblk in
src/decode.cu (the bench default). DSV4_BLK=6 still restores the old arm. Every measurement script
in scripts/ pins its width explicitly in the sweep string, so no frozen protocol moved underneath
this; the head-promotion protocol is now off-policy at 6 and that is written into ladder 2.4.

WHAT WAS MEASURED. Two palindromic sweeps, each inside ONE checkpoint load, on the live s3
checkpoint, adaptK frozen at 1.50, NGEN0 200. Paired per prompt against the shipped BLK=6, bands
2 SE of the per-prompt differences, n=32:

  BLK=5   +0.926 +/- 0.454 tok/s  = +3.91 +/- 1.65 %   26/32 legs   d tau -0.0518 +/- 0.0835
  BLK=4   +0.831 +/- 0.576 tok/s  = +3.78 +/- 2.15 %   23/32 legs   d tau -0.2942 +/- 0.1473
  4 vs 5  -0.095 +/- 0.375 tok/s  = -0.12 +/- 1.43 %                d tau -0.2424 +/- 0.1020

5 is an INTERIOR optimum: 4 buys no throughput over it and does cost acceptance, 6 loses
3.58 +/- 1.53 %, and the first sweep closes everything above -- 7 -1.70, 8 -2.93, 9 -6.30,
10 -9.36, 12 -10.86 %, monotone. tau CLIMBS the whole way down, 3.48 -> 4.36, which is why this
needed ms/token beside it: a wider block buys the metric the project optimises hardest and charges
more than that metric is worth.

WHY. Priced out of the engine's own 13,392 verify rounds (ms_round ~ c0 + cB*BLK + cK*K, fitted per
prompt, banded across prompts): one more DRAFTED position costs +3.324 +/- 0.281 ms, one more
VERIFIED position +15.184 +/- 0.396 ms. The draft forward runs at M=BLK whatever adaptK later
decides, and adaptK stops early far more often than the ceiling suggests -- at BLK=6 the mean
realised verify width is 3.87 of a ceiling of 7 and only 19.3 % of rounds reach it, so 3.13
proposals per round (10.40 ms) are drafted and thrown away. The sixth proposal buys 0.014 tokens
per round and costs 5.40 ms. Round-level ms/token: 43.49 (BLK 4) ~ 43.53 (5) << 45.14 (6), computed
from a different field of the same log than the paired result and agreeing with it to a tenth of a
percent. 5 is also exactly config.json's dspark_block_size -- the width the head was TRAINED at.
F94 pushed serving one position past training while that position was nearly free; 1.1/1.2 ended
that, and DECODE_ZENITH_FINDINGS 3.2 had said in advance that the width would have to be
re-decided or "you will tune to a transient". The transient was 6.

THE ITEM'S OWN PREDICTION WAS WRONG IN THE DIRECTION IT NAMED, and is corrected rather than quietly
dropped: it said the optimum "returns to ~7-9 from an apparent 11-13". It returns to 5, and the
"apparent 11-13" was never a measurement this repo took -- the default was 6 and F94 had already
closed 8. Right mechanism, guessed arithmetic.

WHAT MADE THE FIRST SWEEP UNDER-POWERED WAS NOT REPLICATION. Sweep A gave +2.79 +/- 3.83 % for
BLK=5 -- the right answer with a band straddling zero -- and more replicates would have bought
nothing: repeats of the same (prompt, width) emit a BIT-IDENTICAL id sequence -- 173 mirrored pairs
across the two sweeps, 173 identical, 0 differing -- so a repeat cancels timing drift and nothing else, and sweep B measured that drift at +0.000 +/- 0.002 tok/s over 99
mirrored pairs. The band was between-PROMPT variance; the only estimator that shrinks it is more
prompts. n 8 -> 32 took the band 3.83 -> 1.65 % with the point estimate essentially unmoved. Subsets
agree: suite-8 +2.70 +/- 3.82 %, the 24 held-out prompts +4.32 +/- 1.82 %.

GATES, AND A CLAIM THAT GOT STRONGER WHEN THE READER WAS FIXED. BLK sets M in the verify forward and
therefore MoE atomic reduction order, so this was pre-registered as NOT bit-exact and instrumented
to measure the divergence instead of assuming it. It measures none: LOSSLESS gate 24/24 points PASS
across both sweeps, and across the 344 sequences DSV4_GENOUT recorded, every arm is id-for-id
identical to the BLK=6 reference over every shared position -- 258 comparisons at widths 4 through
12, ZERO differing tokens. The first reading said otherwise because tools/blkwidth_ab.py counted a
different LENGTH as a divergence: generation stops at the first verify that carries the count past
NGEN, so a narrower block overshoots 200 by -6 to +7 tokens and the sequences end at different
positions with equal ids. That is measurement-and-traps 35 run backwards -- 35 compared past the
length at which two arms agree and passed a real difference; this compared past that length and
failed an agreement. The analyser now reports identical / same-ids-different-length / genuinely
different as three columns, and both verdict files were regenerated. The served default
was proven behaviourally, not by reading source: "[engine] loading .../ckpt-head-s3 (seqmax=8192,
blk=5, adaptK=1.50)" from a real run_server.sh load (detached, ppid systemd, tty ?), and
"[parse] point 0: BLK=5" from build/decode with no DSV4_BLK. The four cheap server gates
(tokenizer/encoding/api/stream) pass on the rebuilt binary. No re-measurement was needed after the
rebuild: both arms were measured by the SAME binary in the same load.
